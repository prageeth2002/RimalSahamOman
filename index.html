The company

A trusted partner for Oman's industrial sector since 2007

Rimal Saham Alfidiya Trad & Cont was established in 2007. Over the years we have built a strong client base across both the public and private sectors in Oman.

Our core business is industrial and institutional catering, supported by housekeeping and a broad range of contracting services. We continuously improve our operational processes and make full use of our resources to deliver consistent, reliable service.
