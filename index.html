Who we are

A legacy of quality since 2007

Rimal Saham Alfidiya Trad & Cont was established in 2007. Over the years we have built a strong client base across both the public and private sectors in Oman.

We specialise in industrial and institutional catering, together with housekeeping and a broad range of contracting services, and we keep improving our processes so every client gets consistent, reliable service.

  • Operations aligned with HACCP food-safety guidelines
  • Trained, certified staff at every level
  • Trusted by public and private sector clients
