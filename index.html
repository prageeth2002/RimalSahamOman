About Us

Rimal Saham Alfidiya Trad & Cont was established in 2007. Over the past ten years, we have built an enviable client base among both public and private sectors. Our company primarily deals with industrial and institutional catering activities, including housekeeping.

We are currently focused on improving our operational processes and ensuring optimal utilization of all resources to capture and enhance client satisfaction. Our operations are already under HACCP guidelines.

Customer Satisfaction

Customer satisfaction is our prime goal. As a service provider, quality is the key to customer satisfaction. We have established a robust quality management system to achieve and maintain quality in all functional areas.

Training & Development

Rimal Saham has established a full-fledged training center. We conduct introductory, theoretical, and practical training sessions for all new recruits and existing staff to ensure continuous improvement.

Company Policies

Team Work

Teamwork is the cornerstone of our company. We encourage team spirit, healthy relationships among colleagues, and personal development.

Hard Work & Integrity

We expect hard work, integrity, and honesty from every individual in the company.

Reward & Punishment

We reward employees based on performance and do not hesitate to take action against misconduct or repeated non-performance.
